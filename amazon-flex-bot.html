What Is an Amazon Flex Bot?

An Amazon Flex bot is an app that automates the tedious part of finding delivery work: constantly refreshing the Amazon Flex offer screen and racing to tap "Accept" before someone else does. Instead of staring at your phone for hours, a bot (often called a block grabber) watches for offers on your behalf and accepts the ones you actually want in milliseconds.

The core idea is speed plus selectivity. Good delivery blocks — high pay, a convenient station, the right time slot — get claimed within a fraction of a second of appearing. A human simply can't refresh, read, and tap that fast, consistently, all day. That's the gap an Amazon Flex bot for Android fills.

Why Drivers Use a Flex Bot

Manual block hunting has real costs. Here's what pushes drivers toward automation:

  • Time back: No more sitting in a parking lot mashing the refresh button. Set your filters and let the bot watch.
  • Speed you can't match by hand: The best blocks disappear almost instantly. A bot reacts far faster than a thumb.

In short, drivers use a bot to earn more per hour with less screen time — grabbing the blocks worth their drive and skipping the rest.
