How to Get More Blocks on Amazon Flex

If your Flex schedule keeps coming up empty, you are not doing anything wrong—the offer screen is just brutally competitive. Here is a practical, honest guide to catching more blocks, starting with timing tactics and covering where automation fits (and its real risks).

Learning how to get more blocks on Amazon Flex comes down to two things: understanding why offers vanish, and reducing the time between an offer appearing and you accepting it. Most drivers who feel like they are "never getting blocks" are competing against a first-come release system that rewards fast reactions and good timing. This page walks through the manual tactics that genuinely help, then explains honestly where a tool like FlexPilot fits in—and the real risks that come with any automation.

Why Flex blocks vanish in seconds

Amazon Flex releases blocks on a first-come basis. When a station drops offers, every eligible driver in range sees them at roughly the same moment, and the good ones—higher pay, convenient times, short routes—get claimed almost instantly. That is the core reason people search "amazon flex not getting blocks": you are not slow, you are one of many people all tapping at once.

A few mechanics make it harder:

  • High local demand. Popular metro stations have far more active drivers than available blocks, so the ratio works against you.
  • Refresh timing. The app only shows what it has loaded. If your last refresh was thirty seconds ago, you may be looking at a stale screen while fresh offers appear and disappear.
  • Human tap speed. Even when you see a good block, the gap between spotting it, reading the details, and tapping Accept is often enough for someone else to claim it first.

Understanding this is the whole game. Everything below is about either seeing offers sooner or acting on them faster.

Manual tactics that genuinely help

Before any tool, tighten your own habits. These cost nothing and make a real difference:

  • Refresh at known release windows. Many stations drop the next day's reserved blocks at predictable times, and same-day offers surface throughout the day. Refresh actively during those windows instead of glancing occasionally.
  • Favorite your stations. Setting your preferred warehouses helps the app surface offers relevant to you and cuts noise from locations you would never drive to.
  • Watch surge and off-peak patterns. Pay often rises when demand outstrips drivers—bad weather, holidays, late evenings, and the back half of the week. Off-peak mornings can be quieter but less contested.
  • Know reserved vs. same-day. Reserved blocks are scheduled in advance and tend to be fought over the instant they release. Same-day offers appear closer to the start time and can be easier to catch if you stay ready.
  • Widen your availability sensibly. More eligible time windows and a slightly larger station radius mean more offers reach you in the first place.

Do these consistently and you will see more offers. The remaining bottleneck is speed—the fraction of a second between an offer appearing and Accept being tapped.
