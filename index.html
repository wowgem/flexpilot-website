Smart Block Assistant for Amazon Flex Drivers

FlexPilot helps Amazon Flex drivers monitor block offers and complete schedule selections without continuously tapping the screen.

Operated by Smartpace Technologies Available on Google Play

What FlexPilot Does

FlexPilot is designed for Amazon Flex drivers who find repetitive scrolling and selection physically tiring or who have limited time to monitor the screen.

Block Monitoring

Reads block offers from the Amazon Flex app and surfaces matches based on your configured filters.

Schedule Selection

Performs schedule selections you have configured. You stay in full control — start, stop, and adjust anytime.

Filters & Preferences

Configure preferences for pay rate, station, duration, and time. Only matching blocks are considered.

Privacy First

Reads only the Amazon Flex app. Cannot access other apps, passwords, or personal data.

Account Deletion

App: FlexPilot

Developer: Smartpace Technologies

You have the right to request deletion of your FlexPilot account and all personal data associated with it at any time. We do not currently offer in-app account deletion; deletion is handled by emailing our support address.

To request deletion:

  1. Send an email to support@flexpilot.app from the Google account you use to sign in to FlexPilot.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the Google account email you sign in with so we can verify your identity. No other information is required.

What gets deleted

  • Your user record (email, hashed Android ID, install UUID, app version, sign-in timestamps)
  • Your subscription state, purchase tokens, and trial history
  • Your referral code, redeemed referral relationships, and referral rewards balance
  • Your scan-performance telemetry and crash records associated with your account

Timeline

We action verified deletion requests within 30 days of receipt and email you a confirmation when complete.

What we may retain

A minimal record of the deletion request itself (email, request date, deletion date) for fraud-prevention, tax, and legal-compliance purposes. This retained record contains no in-app activity data and is kept only as long as required by law.

Subscription cancellation

Cancelling your Google Play subscription is a separate action from account deletion — you can cancel via Google Play → Subscriptions → FlexPilot at any time, with or without requesting deletion.
