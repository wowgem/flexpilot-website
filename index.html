Amazon Flex bot & block grabber questions

Is FlexPilot an Amazon Flex bot?

Yes. FlexPilot is an Amazon Flex bot that watches the Flex app for block offers and auto-selects the ones matching your filters, so you never have to tap or refresh manually.

How does the Amazon Flex block grabber work?

Set your preferred pay rate, stations, duration, and time windows once. FlexPilot then monitors the Amazon Flex app in the background and grabs matching blocks in milliseconds as soon as they appear.

How fast does FlexPilot grab blocks?

FlexPilot uses native code paths that process new blocks in milliseconds - fast enough to compete in saturated markets where blocks disappear almost instantly.

How much does the FlexPilot Amazon Flex grabber cost?

FlexPilot is free for 14 days with no card required to start. Refer a friend to extend your trial to 30 days. After the trial, a simple subscription keeps it running.

Is FlexPilot safe for my Amazon Flex account?

FlexPilot only reads the Amazon Flex app to find and select blocks. It cannot access other apps, passwords, or personal data, and you stay in full control - start, stop, or adjust it anytime.
